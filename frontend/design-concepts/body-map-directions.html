The Way of Genius — Body Systems Map

Two ways to make this read as one body

Right now the 11 systems sit as separate blobs placed around a center point — technically organized, but it doesn't read as a body. Both directions below fix that the same way: everything anchors to one silhouette instead of floating apart from it. The difference is what kind of anchor.

OPTION A — LAYERED DISSECTION

Exploded anatomy

One silhouette, pulled apart into layers you can see through — skin, muscle, organs, skeleton — the way it reads at Body Worlds. The nervous system runs as one continuous thread through every layer, which is what keeps it feeling like one body instead of four separate ones.
OPTION B — SACRED GEOMETRY

Radial mandala

One silhouette with the 11 systems placed at the intersections of a real sacred-geometry pattern (flower-of-life rings) centered on the body — geometric and symmetrical, but every point still sits on or inside the body, not scattered around it.
Either way, the controls don't change
Both directions use the same interaction underneath: click a point, a box opens on the right — every time, every layer, no exceptions. Turning the view is left/right/up/down buttons, not a mouse drag. Zoom is +/− buttons. Nothing moves unless you click something. The only thing this page is deciding is which shape the systems live on.
